Sana Farzeen is a senior entertainment journalist who currently leads the Entertainment Bureau at India Today. With over 13 years of experience across print, digital and broadcast journalism, she has previously worked with The Indian Express and the Times Group. She is the recipient of the Best Entertainment Journalist award at the ENBA Awards 2024. Her work spans film criticism, interviews and in-depth stories on the people, trends and business shaping Indian cinema and the wider entertainment industry.
