Through comics and cinema, the cornerstone of the story of men (and women) in spandex is common — it is no cakewalk being a superhero. For decades, our superheroes — even as they have fought fierily and rescued humankind over and over again — have wrestled with self-doubt, isolation, guilt and the pitfalls of leading a double life. All of these feelings explode in the latest Spider-Man outing, with Brand New Day being a crowd-pleasing as well as cerebral film, directed by Destin Daniel Cretton and written by Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers (drawn, of course, from characters originally created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko).
