Given how my heart beats for underdog narratives and anti-system stories, it’s so hard not to praise Meesaya Murukku 2. This is, after all, a film about people trying to get through a door that generations of powerful people have guarded. Talent isn’t enough here. Generations pass, gaana changes, and yet, the struggle remains. That’s why I totally dug the idea of Adhi playing Jeeva, and the young versions of his father and grandfather (senior versions are played by Baba Bhaskar and Karunaas). Beyond the opportunities it provides Adhi to shine, it’s narratively a beautiful emotional choice. It suggests that the years pass, the hairstyles change, the language changes… And yet, the young man trying to get through the door bears the same face. It’s the same dream being denied by the same person.
