It's rare to find a film that leaves every cell in your body vibrating. That was me throughout Takashi Yamazaki's Godzilla Minus Zero. The sequel to his own 2023 Oscar winner, Godzilla Minus One, naturally comes with massive expectations. Yamazaki isn't shying away. He knows exactly what we are hungry for even as he remembers to interrupt with just the precise gut punches. This is a film that knows the might of its proportions. It lavishly serves the thunderous setpieces to audiences while never sacrificing it's profound, devastating emotional core. Embedded in these Kaiju movies is an obvious anti-war appeal. It's all about the nuclear violence mankind has brought on itself by dint of its invasions of natural balances. This film takes it forward by pleading for a break in the cycle of violence. This is an exceptional sequel that's propulsive entertainment married with solid emotional ferocity.
