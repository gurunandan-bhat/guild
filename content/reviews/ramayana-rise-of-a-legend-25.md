Why should our most celebrated and enduring epic require the glitz, glamour and grand spectacle of cinema to make itself accessible to the young, as indeed to the vast multitude of Indians? There are, perhaps, no easy answers to this question, particularly in the wake of the arrival on screen of what is reportedly a ₹4,000-crore (both parts) cinematic undertaking encompassing both instalments. Directed by Nitesh Tiwari, who has co-written the screenplay with Shridhar Raghavan, Ramayana, the first instalment of a proposed two-part saga, aspires to be considerably more than a conventional cinematic experience. Its visual extravagance, underpinned by elaborate visual effects and an imposing production design, invites comparison with some of Hollywood's most ambitious and prohibitively expensive productions.
