‘Why did Karthik Subbaraj have to make Dorothy?’ It is a natural question for anyone who either loved or detested the film. Dorothy is a risk. It is a commercial, public relations and career risk not just for Subbaraj, but also for the cast members — especially the brilliant Rishikanth and Sananth. Which is why it wouldn't be out of place to wonder, 'why'. But then, it is also easy to see that Dorothy isn’t entirely uncharacteristic of Subbaraj. Dorothy is not the kind of film that can just bomb at the box office; that would be the least of its problems.
