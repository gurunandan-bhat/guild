Jason Statham isn’t one to go a-wandering. In _The Beekeeper_ (2024), he plays an ex-Marine out to avenge the death of his landlady. In his next film, _A Working Man_ (2025), he’s an ex-Marine who rescues his employers’ kidnapped daughter. In _Shelter_ (2026), he's an ex-government assassin who protects the daughter of an old friend. And in his latest, _Mutiny_, he plays an ex-Marine out to avenge the death of his employer. The way you can tell them apart is _The Beekeeper_ has a lot of bee-related puns, _A Working Man_ is written by Sylvester Stallone, _Shelter_ is set in the Scottish Hebrides, and _Mutiny_ takes place on a boat.
