Remember Karan Johar’s much-loved franchise ‘Student of the Year’, which was all gloss and romance? In its third edition — in the form of a web series — can the franchise be reimagined to shed the glitter and wade into the darker recesses of being young, and still be irresistible? Strangely enough, as two disparate cinematic universes, Karan Johar’s Dharmatic Entertainment and Reema Maya’s indie and individualistic style of filmmaking, collide, the answer is not confusion or chaos. Reema takes the basic template of ‘SOTY’ and turns it on its head. Yes, it’s still the same elitist St Teresa College, where “privileged children are taught how to struggle”. They are still competing for the elusive Student of the Year trophy. Beyond that, Reema, also the co-writer and co-creator of the series along with Debashish Irengbam, invents her own module and plays by her own rules.
