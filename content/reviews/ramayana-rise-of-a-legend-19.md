It’s a story that is in our collective DNA. Which one of us has not grown up with tales of Ram and Raavan, of good versus evil and the triumph of virtue over sin? Ramanand Sagar immortalised it with his legendary TV series ‘Ramayan’. Om Raut’s ‘Adipurush’ proved that even in a nation where faith is the driving force, ‘Ramayana’ is not the easiest story to tell. Clearly, producer Namit Malhotra and director Nitesh Tiwari had a lot to prove with the first instalment of their two-part ‘Ramayana’.
