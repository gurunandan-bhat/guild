Desperate as hell to look and sound like a film that is serving up a fresh-off-the-oven concoction, Nayyi Navelli, scripted by Divy Nidhi Sharma from a story by Himanshu Sharma, goes overboard with a palette of garish colours and a cast of actors of proven quality bent upon out-hamming each other. The result is a film that neither looks pretty nor sounds right. Talk overshadows action and logic gets drowned out in the confusing din of clashing decibel levels.
