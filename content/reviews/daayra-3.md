“Mera raasta alag hai,” says DCP Ajooni Kohli (Kareena Kapoor Khan) in Daayra as she walks away from DCP Raman Kumar (Prithviraj Sukumaran). He strides in the opposite direction. The camera slips into slow motion, a song kicks in, and the lyrics spell out the obvious: ‘alag raasta’. It is a thriller announcing its metaphor the way a trailer announces a clash, set to a song that is decidedly not ‘Ek Ladki Ko Dekha To Aisa Laga’. That is Meghna Gulzar in 2026: still drawn to the space where law and justice stop being synonyms, still unwilling to let an image sit without a caption.
