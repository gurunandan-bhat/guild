In a film titled Pradhama Drishtya Kuttakkar (prima facie culprits), you’d wonder why its writers spend so much time introducing the police officers. When we first meet Aleena (Parvathy), we’re told that she has lost her husband and that she’s being given his job in the police force on compassionate grounds. She has a baby girl, and it appears that she has no one else to live for except her daughter. In the new station where she takes charge, we also meet Divya (Unnimaya), a senior constable who hasn’t yet been able to conceive a child despite multiple attempts. As for Dasan (Siddharth Bharathan) and Sreenath (Mathew), both of them are on the cusp of starting their respective lives after years of waiting. Everything about these officers and their police station spells out their ordinariness. We catch them picking fights with the egoistic senior officer, and we see them going through their mundane duties without the righteousness of the kind police officers movies are made with. But this is only until these fallible officers collude to make a mistake that’s bigger than all of them. By this point, though, these four have become very real people in our minds, and even their wrongs begin to look a lot like muddy truths to us.
