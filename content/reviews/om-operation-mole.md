A fearless RAW agent goes undercover in Pakistan’s brutal Lyari underworld to dismantle a gangster network... thus read the one-liner of Dhurandhar (2025). Replace Lyari with Red Hills in Chennai, and you almost arrive at the storyline of Dhanush’s latest film O.M: Operation Mole. Directed by Rajkumar Periasamy, who gave us the stirring Amaran, the film follows Thirukumaran (Dhanush), an undercover Special Task Officer. O.M opens with purpose. Nithya Reddy (Sai Pallavi) gets a rousing introduction as an officer at the helm of things who is in pursuit of an illegal sandalwood smuggling racket, and, in particular, the infamous smuggler ‘Burma’ Karthikeyan aka Siya (Mammootty). The first hour or so breezes past, the highlight of which is the single-shot sequence that introduces the protagonist. When we first see Thiru, he is shooting someone, not with a gun, but with a camera. Cops from Andhra Pradesh have arrived to arrest Karthikeyan, but Thiru would have none of it.
