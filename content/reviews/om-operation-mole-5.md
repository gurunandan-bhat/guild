It isn’t hard to draw parallels between O.M: Operation Mole and Mani Ratnam’s Thalapathi (1991), given the roles Mammootty plays in both. In each, he is the boss of a criminal enterprise, and in each, an outsider enters his world: Rajinikanth in Thalapathi, and Dhanush, Rajinikanth’s biggest fan, in the newer film. A dramatic event helps the outsider win trust, and he rises to become the boss’s trusted lieutenant, his thalapathi (commander).  The similarities don’t stop there. The central events of both films unfold during Bhogi, and in both, the boss’s wife shows a particular fondness for the new lieutenant. Thalapathi left us puzzling over the morality of a criminal like Devaraj (Mammootty), and O.M leaves us just as unsure about where Karthikeyan (also Mammootty) stands.
