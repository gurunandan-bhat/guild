It takes a lot to stay level-headed about a bad show, but here goes. Somewhere deep within the edgy-Halloween-party-gone-wrong hollowness of this six-episode riff on Karan Johar’s nepo-induction franchise featuring teeny-bopper colleges and bubblegum love triangles and prestigious annual competitions, there is an idea. It has something to do with the aesthetic of Pink Floyd’s ‘Another Brick in the Wall’: a schooling system so abusive that the Student of the Year contest is an exclusive Squid Game-like tournament with red jumpsuits designed to make unstable Indian teens destroy each other for a mythical survival-of-the-fittest prize. But it’s a hipster idea so blunted by the folds of ivory-tower storytelling and Instagram-filmmaking that, by the time it emerges, it’s an icy corpse floating under a frozen pond.
