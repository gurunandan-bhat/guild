With Digger, Alejandro Gonzalez Innaritu, a multiple Oscar winner (it was five at last count), once again ventures into the territory he is known for — his visceral, psychological filmmaking style exploring gritty realism and raw emotional vulnerability, painful existentialism, all-out redemption, interlinked fates and sweeping stakes. In Digger, he has Tom Cruise for company, with the two taking a wild swing that veers dangerously between ambitious and outlandish. The time is right, more than ever, for a satire on climate change, but Digger is too overstuffed and oddball (not in a good way) to make an impression with lasting impact.
