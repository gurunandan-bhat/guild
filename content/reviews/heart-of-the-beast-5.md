My five minutes of joy — and, more importantly, of peace — are when I lay my head on my furry baby's stomach and talk her through my day. The highs, the lows, the mundane and the momentous... I talk to her about everything. She responds with a few grunts (if I am lucky), but I am mostly greeted with an unstifled yawn. But her eyes tell me she understands. That she shares and cares. That alone is enough to help me power through yet another day.
