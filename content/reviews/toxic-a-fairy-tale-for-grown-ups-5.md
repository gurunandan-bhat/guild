A love so raw, it hurts. Everybody in the vicinity. And there are guns ready to blast off all around. Drama that emerges after an hour of confused mayhem finds tearful fiancee/bride Rebecca (Tara Sutaria), Raya (Yash) oozing uncontrolled testosterone, an extramarital passion with Circuswali Nadia (Kiara Advani) and Raya’s sister Ganga (Nayanthara) who’s sold herself early to take care of bro’s hungry tummy. But, as a line in the film goes, men have no brains below the tummy. On the sidelines cackles blood thirsty rival gangster Elizabeth (Huma Qureshi). A somewhat watchable story gets gnarled with blurred timelines, fragmented scenes and a hero who rasps rather than talks.
