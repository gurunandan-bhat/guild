Mirzapur perhaps marks the first instance of a popular web series being turned into a feature film. The series, which ran for three successful seasons over the last eight years, was helmed by Gurmeet Singh, who has also directed the film. Much of the team remains the same, from the cinematographer to the actors, with the notable exception of Vikrant Massey, replaced here by Jitendra Kumar. Kumar is a fine actor and fits the bill perfectly.
