The gripping courtroom thriller centres on Ujjwal Nikam (Rajkummar Rao), India’s celebrated public prosecutor, revisiting his formidable legal battle against Ajmal Kasab, the sole surviving gunman captured during the 26/11 Mumbai terror attacks. Tasked with securing justice by proving Kasab’s guilt beyond reasonable doubt, Nikam faces unprecedented challenges and makes immense personal sacrifices to build an airtight case in a trial that holds the nation’s attention. He handled the Kasab case from December 2008 until Kasab’s execution in November 2012, spanning nearly four years. The 26/11 terror attacks didn’t just shake the nation and the world; they drove a dagger into the beating heart of Mumbai, a city whose indomitable spirit and resilience have always defied its darkest hours. It remains one of the most painful chapters in the city’s history, one that continues to haunt and unsettle every Mumbaikar.
