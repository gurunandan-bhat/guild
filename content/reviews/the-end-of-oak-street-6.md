The End Of Oak Street is the most fun I’ve had in a theatre this year. David Robert Mitchell’s dino fare brings ample suspense and heart into the vestiges of blockbuster commercial cinema trappings. It’s strewn with sequences that set my pulse racing and had me almost on the brink of whistles. Honestly, I have to scratch my memory real hard to even recall when a mainstream film got me jiving to its wavelength from the get go and refused to loosen the shackles. The End Of Oak Street is the definition of a proper, thrilling communal experience to get a film that exults in splendidly orchestrated moments. My regret is the theatre was much too empty to feel the ripples bounce across the breadth of the audience. Where are the moviegoers? You have been summoned.
