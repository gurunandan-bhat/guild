When the audience is introduced to Dhanush in OM, in the character of Thirukumaran or Thiru, he stands atop a makeshift scaffolding, scanning the vibrant crowd at a local temple festival. His focus remains on his employer, Burma Karthikeyan 'Siya', played by Mammootty, who is the chief guest of the event. The entire sequence, shot to look like a continuous take (reminiscent of the opening scene of the French drama Athena), follows an attempt by Andhra Pradesh police to arrest Karthikeyan for illegal red sanders smuggling. Even before Karthikeyan arrives, his henchmen, including an area councillor, make the police force run for their lives.
