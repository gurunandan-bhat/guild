“This isn’t an Amar Chitra Katha fairytale” says Rocking Star Yash in the introduction to his first film since KGF: Chapter 2, the movie that turned his fortunes around by giving him pan-India popularity. It’s another thing that by the end of three hours and 15 minutes of crass dialogues, gory violence and over-the-top shenanigans in Toxic, one yearns for a simple Amar Chitra Katha tale. Action and swagger are key attributes of Yash’s screen persona. In Toxic, though, there’s overreliance on the combo to such an extent that the story seems to have been forgotten altogether. Like the KGF films, here too it’s hard to keep pace with what is happening, where it is unfolding and even who is who after a point.
