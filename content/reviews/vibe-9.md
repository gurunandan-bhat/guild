There will be no sophomore slump in Kunal Kemmu’s directorial career, which began with the sleeper hit comedy Madgaon Express (2024). His second movie is called Vibe, and this title is by far the worst thing about it — aside from, perhaps, its bloated length. They explain the meaning behind the title right at the end of its 145-minute run-time — it’s an acronym; surprise, surprise — but there's no denying that it makes the movie sound like a rooftop bar in Delhi’s Hauz Khas. Or worse, an elder millennial’s desperate attempt to attract a young audience.  Vibe is neither a rooftop bar nor is it in need of any publicity gimmicks. It’s a movie that should succeed on positive word-of-mouth alone. A smartly directed and impeccably performed action comedy, the film more than earns a place alongside Go Goa Gone (2013) and Madgaon Express in a spiritually connected trilogy about fools who find themselves in sticky situations.
