When noted filmmaker Sangeeth Sivan passed away in 2024, he left behind a rich legacy of genre-defining Malayalam films such as Yoddha and Nirnayam alongside a string of unapologetic Hindi potboilers. His posthumous thriller, Blind Game, arrives as an intriguing final chapter, attempting to channel the taut, puzzle-box sensibility of his 1990s thrillers into a Hindi adaptation of the Malayalam hit 7th Day. Yet, despite strong screen presence from Arjun Rampal and Priyamani, the film struggles to match the razor-sharp craft of his early career, leaving both the plot and its characters lost in the mist of the Himachal hills.
