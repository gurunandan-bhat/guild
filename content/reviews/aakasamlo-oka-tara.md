There are times when we yearn for a story of hope. Told well, it can take us back to the innocence of childhood, when we would not hesitate to dream. Aakasamlo Oka Tara (A Star in the Sky), written by Gunnam Gangaraju and directed by Pavan Sadineni, is one such story. Starring Dulquer Salmaan, newcomer Satvika Veeravalli and Shruti Haasan, this underdog tale is told with enough conviction to make us root for its characters rather than dwell on its predictability. The writing, performances and technical craft come together to deliver one of the best Telugu films in recent times.
