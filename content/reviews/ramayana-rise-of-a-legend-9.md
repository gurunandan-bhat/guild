There are few stories as extraordinary, timeless and deeply rooted in Indian culture as the Ramayana. Bringing an epic of this scale to the big screen is no small task, especially when millions of people have their own emotional and spiritual connection with its characters. Director Nitesh Tiwari takes on that challenge with a film that is visually spectacular, emotionally engaging and technically accomplished. The first thing that strikes you about Ramayana is its sheer scale. With the visual effects expertise of the artists who have worked on films like The Odyssey, Dune and Interstellar, expectations are naturally high. Thankfully, the film delivers on that front. Forests, kingdoms, animals, gods, demons, rakshasas, mythical creatures and massive battle sequences come alive through some impressive visual effects. The world-building is ambitious, colourful and immersive. You can see the enormous effort that has gone into creating this cinematic universe.
