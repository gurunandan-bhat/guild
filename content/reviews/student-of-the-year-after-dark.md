Almost one-and-a-half decade since Karan Johar's Student of the Year entered our lives and envisioned college as a swanky theme park for affluent, entitled kids where a token outsider would act as disruptor and stir the status quo whilst participating in a medley of designer labels and fancy musicals. Student of the Year is as frivolous as it gets but grabbed eyeballs for embracing its vanity to the hilt.
