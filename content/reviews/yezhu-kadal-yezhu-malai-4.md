What’s the name of Nivin Pauly’s character in Yezhu Kadal Yezhu Malai? It’s a question you’re likely to grapple with as you watch the film that’s narrated as a story to a little boy by his aunt on what appears to be a boring train journey. To this boy, Nivin’s character goes by Hulk, an all-powerful superhero who has been alive for centuries. As for Soori, the other central character in this story, Nivin goes by Sir at first, until Soori begins to call him Karuppiah, the name of his village deity. As for the viewer, we’re told that this character has been immortal for millennia, drifting from one phase of history to the next in search of the woman he’s loved since he first saw her, 4,000 years ago.
