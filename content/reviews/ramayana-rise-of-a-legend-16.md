Director Nitesh Tiwari’s Ramayana Part 1: Rise Of A Legend starts off when Asuras face a drubbing defeat by the Devas. Hence, they live in extreme poverty and helplessness in Lanka while Kubera rules over them. Dashanana’s (Raghav Malhotra) mother Kaikasi (Shobana) is sad to see him unperturbed by their condition and encourages him to do something for the rise of the Asuras. Hence, Dashanana carries out intense penance for years to please Lord Brahma and gets a boon of almost immortality. He then becomes the mighty, powerful and ruthless king of Lanka, Ravana (Yash).
