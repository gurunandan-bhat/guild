In 2018, with his sophomore film Ee Nagaraniki Emaindi (ENE), director Tharun Bhascker Dhaassyam proved that his breakout film Pelli Choopulu was no fluke, breaking the second-film jinx that plagued young filmmakers at the time. An urban buddy comedy about aspiring filmmakers, ENE gradually won over young audiences with its characters, sharp humour, technical finesse and music, eventually earning cult status. Sequels to much-loved films are tricky, but ENE Repeat does not squander its potential. It reaffirms Tharun’s distinct voice as a writer and filmmaker.
