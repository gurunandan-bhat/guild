Toxic is 3 hrs 14 mins long.If you remove all the shots of the lead played by Yash walking around in slow motion - you’re down to about 2 hours 30 minutes. Remove all the shots of Yash smoking a cigar/cigarette - around 2 hours. Remove the violent fights involving axes, guns, knives, grenades, chainsaws and what appears to be the entire inventory of a hardware store — and you’re left with roughly 60 minutes. Which, frankly, would have been the perfect length, because the entire film is summarised in the last 20 minutes anyway.
