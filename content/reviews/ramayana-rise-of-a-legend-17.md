The litmus test for how I felt about Nitesh Tiwari’s Ramayana: Part 1 was its action sequences, or more accurately, how I kept nodding off during them. Sure, one could argue that a 6.30 am show and a mild coffee might be partially responsible. Tiwari has never made anything in the realm of a visual spectacle to be hailed for his imagination (not counting Dangal, which had a few nifty wrestling sequences). And yet, I was surprised by how non-discernible the fights felt. I get it, working with green-screen, VFX, stars, and such a large canvas can be daunting – but I couldn’t ignore how dull it all felt – like pixels at war with themselves. Maybe it was also my familiarity with the source material here, which has been an inspiration for so many mainstream family dramas.
