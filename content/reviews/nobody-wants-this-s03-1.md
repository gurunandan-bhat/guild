It's time to say mazel tov! Kristen Bell's Joanne and Adam Brody's Noah are now living together, and the former decides to take the next big steps in her conversion to Judaism. The Netflix rom-com had a shaky second season where the couple questioned their relationship status, but now, their bond is stronger than ever. Based on creator Erin Foster's real-life experiences, Nobody Wants This Season 3 also explores the connection between Joanne and Noah's siblings, Sasha (Timothy Simons) and Morgan (Justine Lupe). From family interferences to sibling tiffs, the new instalment has plenty of fun and chaotic sequences.
