He drinks, she smokes. He sings, she heckles. He eats, she cooks. He calls her a mad woman, she calls him a wastrel. He criticises her food, she whines about being stuck with him. Bickering is not only their only means of communication, it is also their love language. Or whatever you call “love” after 65 years of marriage. They don’t speak, they mutter to themselves so that the other can hear. Padam and Tulsi are rusty habits to each other. They may sound like a crowd-pleasing romcom tale, but they’re actually life companions browbeaten by time and tide. Misery loves company, but their company is fuelled by misery; he enjoys winding her up, she is affectionate in her meanness.
