Lucknow is clearly the new flavour of the season: ‘Parivarik ManuRanjan’ which takes its job of being a ‘family-oriented film’ very seriously indeed, is the third or fourth recent film set in UP’s historic capital. But it’s also an enterprise which has taken to heart the recent insistence on the city’s vegetarian version, given just how lacking the two romantic leads are in hearty red blood cells. Pankaj Tripathi and Aditi Rao Hydari, let loose in a large kothi in the city’s well-known ‘Chowk’ area, are the very epitome of wholesomeness, without a single lustful thought crossing their minds.
