Every franchise outing is sandwiched between nostalgia and innovation. There's an impulse to dial back to earlier entries, coast on past glory. Clashing with it is the pressing desire to widen stakes and introduce new frontiers. A standout emerges when it truly distils a measure of both. Francis Lawrence steered most of Jennifer Lawrence's The Hunger Games adaptations. His return to helm Sunrise On The Reaping, based on Suzanne Collins' eponymous 2025 prequel, yields a film that's dynamic and dull by turns. However, one thing is certain. Its lead actor Joseph Zada is a bona fide star. He has the kind of singular charisma that keeps you rapt even when the film goes rudderless.
