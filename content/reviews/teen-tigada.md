One of the graver crimes committed by Indian streaming is giving modern or ‘brave’ themes the television-soap treatment. Actually, calling it television-soap treatment is reductive — and generous. It’s a crutch we use to describe algorithmic shows that have no personality. So what I really mean is this is flat storytelling bereft of curiosity and character; it seeks viewers looking for content, and therefore sets out to balance its new-age core with the most facile filmmaking possible. The fillers of a city look fake, the visuals look AI-generated, and everyone behaves like an emotion-bot; it’s a throwback to those early Voot and ALTBalaji shows, where every setting looks like a glitchy simulation of reality.
