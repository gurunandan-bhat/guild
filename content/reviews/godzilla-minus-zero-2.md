Epic Kaiju action bolstered by fabulous VFX and generous servings of human emotion lift Godzilla Minus Zero tantalisingly close to the heights that its predecessor, Godzilla Minus One, scaled in 2023. The film does enough to be deemed a worthy follow-up to a hard act to follow. The first film conquered the world, mopped up big box-office bucks and landed a Best Visual Effects Academy Award for director Takashi Yamazaki, only the second filmmaker in history after Stanley Kubrick (2001: A Space Odyssey) to win an Oscar in that category.  Godzilla Minus Zero adds up to a great deal as it delivers more of the same without letting any of its monster swings come across as dull and predictable. As one watches the spectacle unfurl its wares across a runtime of two hours and a quarter, you cannot help wondering if there is another Oscar lurking somewhere in here for all the snazzy wizardry.
