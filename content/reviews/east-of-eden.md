In 1955, Elia Kazan directed the James Dean starrer, East of Eden, which followed the last part of John Steinbeck's semi-autobiographical 1952 novel. Dean played Cal Trask, who fights for his father's love alongside his perfect brother Aron. A new Netflix limited series on the material, written and showrun by Elia's granddaughter, Zoe Kazan, goes deep into the novel, exploring the Biblical themes, characters and the promise of America at the turn of the century. Backed by a strong cast, East of Eden follows the doomed marriage of Adam (Christopher Abbott) and Cathy (Florence Pugh) that spills over its sins to the next generation. Finely acted, the ambitious and largely faithful period drama really gets into the characterisation from the novel, while also immersing viewers into its self-righteous world.
