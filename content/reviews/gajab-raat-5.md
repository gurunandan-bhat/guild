Conscience awakens, corruption collapses and justice is served over the course of a night in Ritesh Shah's Gajab Raat. Co-written by him and Producer Neeraj Pandey, Shah's directorial debut captures flawed characters and grim realities through social structures and sardonic eyes leaving Manoj Bajpayee to hold the fort. There's no cinematic style or technical wizardry to be found in Shah's storytelling. Dialogues are his strong suit and he uses peppery exchanges to fire up drama on OTT screen. Inspired by the Neeraj Pandey school of corridor strolls, the screenplay engages in prolonged scenes of walking and talking on phone and in person at hotels and hospitals.
