Villainous godmen are becoming an OTT staple. The acclaim of Aashram (2020), which catapulted Bobby Deol’s dangling career, was followed by the rockstar act of Manoj Bajpayee in Sirf Ek Bandaa Kaafi Hai (2023) where he played a lawyer, going against a godman to ensure justice to a rape victim. There was also Netflix's Maharaj (2024), which again revolved around a baba brainwashing and sexually assaulting his female disciples. Godmen make for great antagonists as they provide filmmakers with a controversy-free proxy to comment on the banes of blind-faith, callousness of power systems and the perils of patriarchal societies, while also championing the need for female liberty and free thinking.
