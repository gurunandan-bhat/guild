We are in hi-fi hell,” a character squeaks out somewhere at the halfway mark of Student of the Year: After Dark. I sit back in my seat and squirm, well aware that even if I tried, I couldn’t have summed it up better. Clearly, trading dopamine-tinged visuals and candy-floss romance for a moody, gothic-pop aesthetic isn’t quite the way to update a franchise for a generation whose youngest members were born in the same year as the release of the first Student of the Year film. SOTY is now a series. After Dark, whose six episodes are streaming on JioHotstar, has the distinct goal of wanting to speak to Gen-Z, but the fundamental flaw in its depiction of this demographic is such that it ends up misunderstanding, and often, misrepresenting it.
