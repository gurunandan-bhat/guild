Meghna Gulzar showed her penchant for balancing two different viewpoints when she made the largely true-life Talvar (2015) with Vishal Bhardwaj as writer. Although this time it is a medley of cases that she mixes without picking any single one, Meghna’s flair for effectively presenting contrasting opinions continues as DCP Raman Kumar (Prithviraj Sukumaran) revels in public applause. Four monsters behind the grisly rape and murder of a charred body have been gunned down by the police. Self-defence, say Raman Kumar and his team. Let’s investigate, says DCP Ajooni Kohli (Kareena Kapoor Khan), introduced as a fearless cop heading a vigilance unit that keeps a check on the police. She has just busted a racket where superintendents fawn over VIP prisoners, serving them a special menu of quinoa pulao and avocado.
