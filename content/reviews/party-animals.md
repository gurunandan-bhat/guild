A bad film doesn’t become good if it knows it’s bad. Like acknowledging your stupidity won’t make you smart. The marketing for Party Animals has had this self-deprecating energy. The trailers labelled it as “definitely not the movie of the year”, one of its leads Viraj Ghelani was described as “that gujju guy from Instagram.” It sells an impression that the makers are smart as they made a bad film while knowing that it is bad. Sadly, meta-ness doesn’t suffice for content.
