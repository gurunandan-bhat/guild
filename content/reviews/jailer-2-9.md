Rajinikanth’s Jailer 2 is a thoroughly unhinged and deeply misogynistic sequel that trades a coherent plot for a never-ending parade of celebrity cameos and slow-motion walking. The film’s baffling second half transforms Rajinikanth’s character into a CIA-level director operating at Fast & Furious levels of absurdity. We discuss the frustratingly low bar for mass masala entertainers, particularly the script's reliance on outdated male gaze tropes, item songs, and bizarrely exposing costumes for female fighters. Additionally, we poke holes in the film’s 1960s-era James Bond villain logic, where antagonists willingly explain their plans. We also wonder if the franchise has completely lost its emotional core, relying entirely on its addictive background music and an Om Shanti Om-style conveyor belt of guest stars to keep audiences engaged.
