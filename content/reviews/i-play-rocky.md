A powerful screenplay, a determined rookie actor, and an origin story that would become film history. Peter Farrelly’s I Play Rocky goes beyond this one-liner synopsis, capturing the irony of a legend that seems like it wrote itself, even though Stallone had to bleed for every single page. What really stuns you is the amount of faith the studio had in a script (of the 1976 blockbuster Rocky) from a total unknown. Watching it from India (where writers are routinely discarded from trailer end slates and their scripts are constantly reworked), you feel the weight of Stallone’s decision not to sell his work unless he was going to play the lead in a movie that would make history.
