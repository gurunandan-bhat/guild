Speaking broadly, the structure upon which Nelson builds both his Jailer films is strictly pedestrian. They’re based on uncovering complex trade networks exporting priceless sculptures from Southern India. They’re also written around the tension that arises from discovering the powers that the next big boss, Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, must encounter as he climbs the corporate ladder of this idol theft enterprise. If Jailer was all about Muthuvel taking on the foot soldiers and middle management, the stakes only get bigger in this film as he schemes against those at the top of the pyramid.
