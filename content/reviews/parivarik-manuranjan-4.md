The quiet life of Ranjan Dubey (Pankaj Tripathi), a kind-hearted 40-something bachelor in Lucknow, changes when he hires a mysterious woman named Helen (Aditi Rao Hydari) as his live-in house manager. As romance blossoms, town gossip and Helen’s past threaten to test their bond. The casting is generally apt, though, much like the storytelling, it plays things a little too safe and risk-averse, denying the film some spunk and character. Pankaj Tripathi  bags a role that fits his persona to a T, and he breezes through it with ease. Aditi Rao Hydari, ever since Kaatru Veliyidai/Cheliya, has increasingly been cast in roles where she plays a woman waiting to be saved by a man. Though she does well here, the repetition in her choices hurts the impact. Brijendra Kala and Sunita Rajwar lend warmth to the film in familiar roles that do not break new ground. Mukesh Tiwari, meanwhile, does not leave a lasting impression.
