It’s quite easy to describe _I’m Game_, Nahas Hidhayath’s follow up to his wildly entertaining debut, _RDX_ (2023). Imagine if SP Muthuraman’s _Enakkul Oruvan_ (1984) and Madonne Ashwin’s _Maaveeran_ (2023) had a baby. The older film is a kooky reincarnation saga played with melodramatic earnestness while the latter maintains an irony-tipped sharpness through its serious action-oriented storytelling. _I’m Game_ doesn’t always get the ratio of this mix right, but it hedges its bets on the handful of inventive ideas that keeps the proceedings engaging.
