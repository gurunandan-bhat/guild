A trivia question for the ages: what film has Monica Bellucci, Giancarlo Esposito, Martin Lawrence, Salman Khan and Sanjay Dutt in its cast? The answer is 7 Dogs, a Saudi Arabian action thriller by Adil El Arbi and Bilall Fallah, the directing duo behind the last two Bad Boys movies. The internationalism is more or less the point. Admittedly, there's always a cheap thrill in watching your favourite faces in a snazzy global production, complete with explosions and gunfights. It's an old tradition across media. Think of it as the cinematic equivalent of an all-stars charity match — sans the charity.
