At the 90-minute mark, when emotions are running high and the tension in this investigative spy thriller is begging for a grand hero-elevation moment, you expect Dhanush to rise, take charge and deliver a high. But director Rajkumar Periyasamy does something rather unexpected. Instead of building his protagonist into an invincible hero, he strips him of that very image, culminating in an emotionally devastating sequence that pushes him to the brink of a mental breakdown. And just like that, O.M: Operation Mole tells you it is playing by a very different set of rules. This isn't your typical police procedural drama.
