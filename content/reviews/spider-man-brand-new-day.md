Tom Holland's Peter Parker grows up in Brand New Day, a melancholic, grief-stricken Spider-Man story that trades humour for humanity. Following the events of Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021), New York's friendly neighborhood hero is no longer a bumbling teenager. With his high school years behind him, Peter Parker enters a new chapter of his life, confronting its defining realities—grief, loss, loneliness, and a profound journey of self-reflection. Having lost Aunt May and the two people closest to him, Ned and MJ, whose memories of Peter have been erased, he is left to navigate adulthood alone. Now a full-time masked vigilante, he fights crime while battling the isolation within. Melancholy lingers where humor once prevailed. Already wrestling with his inner demons, Peter is forced to take on a formidable nemesis who can manipulate the minds of others. (Hint: the origin of an iconic X-Men character played by Sadie Sink).
