There are many bad films. I've seen bad films week after week. But there are bad films, and then there are really bad films. And then there is something like Geetu Mohandas' Yash-starrer Toxic. The biggest problem with Toxic is not the lack of resources. In fact, it's the opposite. The makers had everything you could possibly need to make a big, ambitious film: a superstar, a producer with money, a fantastic technical team, some of the biggest actresses, good music, huge sets, international locations and all the money required to create a spectacle. What they didn't have was a good story.
