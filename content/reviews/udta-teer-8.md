Can you imagine a spy comedy that uses cross-border tensions, mistaken identities and political undertones to deliver laughs? Udta Teer, directed by Aakash Kaushik, starts on a high note and promises a thoroughly entertaining ride. The film gets its comic timing right early on, and the first half is genuinely hilarious. Unfortunately, it struggles to maintain that momentum after the interval. The biggest strength of Udta Teer is its writing in the first half. The situations are funny, the dialogue often lands, and several scenes are smartly constructed around the political context without making the humour feel forced. The film knows the absurdity of its premise and makes good use of it. There are moments when the comedy is sharp, unexpected and genuinely laugh-out-loud funny.
