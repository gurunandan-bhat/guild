Kiran Rao is a talented filmmaker. She is also an incredibly clever storyteller. The latter is evident, and palpably so, in her segment of Lust Stories 3. The second film in Netflix's latest instalment of its four-in-one anthology — that has looked at relationships, sex and (mostly female) desire through an often cheeky, mostly empathetic and sometimes outlandish lens, over three outings — is the one that gets the memo most accurately. Rao's film doesn't have a name. None of the four in Lust Stories 3 does, except being interconnected by certain timelines, which may or may not intersect. They also may or may not make sense.
