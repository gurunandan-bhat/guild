A bunch of dim-wits. Crores of money. A villain desperately hunting for a pen drive or a key. Police officers and investigators who invariably arrive at the scene only after everything has already happened. Several heist dramas have adorned the big screen over the years. Will CM Vijay’s son, Jason Sanjay, deliver something new with this heist drama titled Sigma built around new-age ideas? Let’s find out.
