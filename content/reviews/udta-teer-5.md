It looked too good to be true. A mainstream hero of a stirred-up democracy championing a contentious padosi mulk's cause is as brave as Ayushmann Khurrana's oeuvre of out-of-the-box characters gets. But Udta Teer's flight of fantasy goes only thus far and no further. When the choice is between Pakistani intelligence and Indian patriotism, the verdict is as clear as day. All works should be seen as a whole but writer Akash A Kaushik's directorial debut is akin to watching two movies in one -- the first half is doused in goofy irreverence having a ball without batting an eyelid, the other is an exercise in appeasement at the cost of jarring tonal shifts.
