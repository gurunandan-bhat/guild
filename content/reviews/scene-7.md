Scene sets itself up instantly with a scene at a graveyard. On a rainy night, men are trying to get rid of a body, only to run into a young murderer. He is Eeswaran (a terrific Naslen) who, while tied to a cross, bargains his release by promising them more kills in the near future. “At least once in life somebody wants to kill someone. I will do it for you,” he declares. Eeswaran, audiences are told, has no motivation other than to kill for fun. With a foe so quirky, it is imperative then that the man (police officer) who will bring him down have his own set of idiosyncrasies. Trust Malayalam film director Jithu Madhavan to deliver in his Tamil film debut.
