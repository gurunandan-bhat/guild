Patriotism is a difficult sentiment to practise in India today. Unlike religion, which you can choose to keep private, patriotism is something you are often expected to perform in public. The social pressure is such that you might invite a rude stare if you don’t shout “Bharat Mata Ki Jai” after the National Anthem in a cinema hall, which is perhaps the least patriotic of places. Director Akash A Kaushik’s Udta Teer raises a tricky question in this climate: is love for one’s homeland interchangeable? The film’s recurring line, “mulk ke aage kuch nahin”, makes us wonder which nation truly belongs to whom, and who gets to decide what patriotism should look like.
