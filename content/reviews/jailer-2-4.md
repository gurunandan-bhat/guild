_Black and Blue_, the fifth episode of the sixth season of Vince Gilligan's _Better Call Saul_, begins—like a lot of cold openings typical of Gilligan's work—with a silent montage of an artisan encasing a slide rule in a block of clear acrylic. The engraving reads, "In Liebe… Deine Jungs" ("With love… your boys"), and the piece is packed into a wooden box. (It becomes a cardinal sin to invoke the series without mentioning how criminally underwatched it is. So, here you go: it is criminally underwatched.)  If you've managed to watch up to this episode, you already know that this setup will have a massive payoff. You also know that you will completely forget about it within a few minutes, as you get engrossed in the incredible writing of one of modern-day long-form geniuses. But when the payoff hits, the memory of the setup rains down on you!
