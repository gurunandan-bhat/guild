Akka on Netflix is unlike anything you've seen before. Set in an unusual world where women call the shots, this series creates a universe governed by power, loyalty, fear and legacy. It is a society where women don't just occupy positions of authority; they fiercely protect what belongs to them, passing down their influence and traditions from one generation to the next. But every empire has its vulnerabilities, and when an outsider arrives to challenge a legacy that has ruled Pernuru for years, the carefully guarded world begins to unravel. At the centre of this conflict are two formidable women. Bhavani, played by Keerthy Suresh, is a powerful figure whose authority and legacy come under threat from Rudra, portrayed by Radhika Apte. Rudra is a ruthless rebel with her own ambitions, and she is determined to challenge the established order and claim what she believes is rightfully hers. What follows is a gripping battle of egos, ambition, revenge and survival, with the two women refusing to back down.
