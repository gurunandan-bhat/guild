David Ayer last directed Brad Pitt in Fury (2014), a moody, muddy drama about a WWII tank commander and his wearying crew. Despite the excellent cast — imagine riding along killing Nazis with Michael Peña, Shia LaBeouf and Jon Bernthal — it was nobody's idea of a buddy adventure. Prospects are sunnier in Ayer's latest film, Heart of the Beast, which cuts back on human players and pairs a 62-year-old Pitt with a dog. Not just any dog, mind you, but a ferocious US Special Forces canine. He’s played by a swarthy German Shepherd named Uber, and there are lovely shots of Pitt massaging his fingers on the beast's hair. An alternative title for the film could be Furry.
