The first fifteen minutes of Blind Game are ominous. And I’m not talking about the story. The film-making is so dated that it made me feel like a child again. The dubbing is very 1980s: the foley effects are almost a character. You can hear the studio-produced footsteps and the synthetic breathing of a man being chased in the jungle. A gunshot sounds like a human mouth imitating the whizz of a bullet (“dishkiyaoon!”). A cop taking on a bunch of crooks on a Manali road lands punches with a distinct “dishoom!”. The fundamental nature of the craft is reduced to a punchline. If it weren’t unintentional, it’d be nostalgic.
