If Tiger Shroff dressed up in a Spiderman suit in SOTY 2 wasn’t enough horror, now we have Student Of The Year: After Dark, a twisted turn on the otherwise sunshine young-adult romance franchise. There are no dance numbers introducing the characters or chiffon sarees swaying against white, cold hills. In fact, the central character whose death rolls the series is fished out of an ice lake, drowned and frozen. Darkly ironic.
