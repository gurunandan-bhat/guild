Netflix has made a killing out of Harlan Coben. At last count, the American writer had as many as 13 titles running on the streaming platform, with each generating massive watch hours. Case in point: The adaptation of Coben’s I Will Find You, released this year, accumulated 619 million hours watched (equating to over 112.9 million views) during its historic run on Netflix. Coben’s writing is formulaic and functional, with many describing it as “airport fiction”, the kind that employs relentless plot twists at the cost of literary depth. But in a world ruled by decreasing attention span, low patience for world-building and the preference for page-turning, high-velocity reading, Coben thrives.
