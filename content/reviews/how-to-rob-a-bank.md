How to Rob a Bank movie review: There’s usually something quite irresistible about watching a bunch of Robin Hood types intent upon stealing from the rich to give to the poor, especially if the thieves have soft-touch backstories. So here are your Pittsburgh-based robbers– Ryan (Nicholas Hoult), his wife Emi (Anna Sawai) , war veteran Vince (Pete Davidson) and tech wizard Hugo (Rheny Feliz)– who each have an excuse to do what they are doing. But because this is an Amazon-backed ‘original’ which has to compete with endless scrolls on multiple platforms for eyeballs, it becomes more a memefest rather than a movie. This is what happens when your thieves are more interested in gathering followers online, rather than going after cold cash: their pursuits are bookended by cool graphics and cooler fonts, wolf masks through which eyes gleam, and jazzy tunes.
