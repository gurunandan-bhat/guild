At a time when journalism is reduced to either fact-checking or absolute sycophancy hadn’t we become pessimistic enough to jettison that starry-eyed reporter who “fought for the truth”? This constant conscience-invoking journalist, who barges into meetings to give it back to his seniors, who calls their colleagues “cowards” for not chasing a story, who doesn’t flinch even when he is fired, is a unicorn who exists only in the movies. But here me out, I am not saying there are no truth-seeking journalists, there are, but things are not as simplistic as it is shown in Reporting Live. I had to do a massive suspension of reality when I saw a character filing an RTI application in one scene, and getting the answers in the next one.
