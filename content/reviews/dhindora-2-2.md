I’m always a bit puzzled when faced with brainrot comedy. Otherwise known as cringe humour. Otherwise known as leave-your-brains-at-home entertainment (if it’s streaming, I’m watching it in the very home I’m supposed to leave my brains at; go figure). Otherwise known as gag-a-minute, crackpot or lowbrow satire. You get the gist. It’s kind of a fool-proof genre. If I find it idiotic and exhausting, it’s done its job; if I roll my eyes, it’s done its job; if I find it unwatchable, it’ll claim to have accomplished its goal. So if it’s bad, it’s actually…good? And if it’s funny, well, that’s a bonus.
