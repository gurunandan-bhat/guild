Of course, _Toxic_ has a narrator. How can it not? How can it not have the lazy storytelling device that's quintessential of this brand of film that's basically 'stuti puranam' (hymn of adoration) of the protagonist. How can we just have an introduction scene of Yash without a narration in the background that is all talk and no sense. How can he make a direct entry, without teasing the fans with glimpses of his hands, neck tattoo and cigar, before finally revealing his face. So, yes, _Toxic_ has a narrator, the bazooka gun, small guns, big guns, big cigars, small joints, women, more women, beheadings, blood splattering, mutilated hands, mutilated legs and for good measure, also another Yash! What it doesn't have is the depth and style it so badly attempts at.
