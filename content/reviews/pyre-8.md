Padam Singh (Padam Singh Kapri) and Tulsi (Hira Devi) might be the last two people on the planet. Or so it seems, in the remote hills around Pithoragarh, in Uttarakhand’s Kumaon region, where the couple has lived most of their life. Their son, Hariya, left home about 30 years ago, who they’ve not heard from since. All their neighbours have slowly packed their lives into a few pieces of luggage, and left the hills. The few neighbours, who help the elderly couple in their time of need, are also slowly making their way to a nearby town (Haldwani).  Padam, 84 and Tulsi, 75, might be relatively active for their age – but as people keep leaving the village, a calamity seems around the corner. An hour away from the nearest pliable road, and then another two hours away from the nearest village clinic, any moderate ailment might mean a death sentence for them. In that case, what do they do? End their lives? Or wait for the end?
