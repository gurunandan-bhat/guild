Parivarak ManuRanjan is about a soft 40-year-old bachelor, Ranjan Dubey (Pankaj Tripathi), who grows feelings for his new housekeeper and property manager, Helen (Aditi Rao Hydari). He is single because he spent his life getting his sisters married off; his duty is over, he works at the electricity board and lives alone. The story unfolds in the gossipy bylanes of Lucknow. If you close your eyes and think of a nosy neighbour, it’s a failure of Hindi cinema that Sunita Rajwar instantly comes to mind. Blame this setting and voice-over on Gullak, the virtuous protagonist on Rab Ne Bana Di Jodi and Raksha Bandhan, and the thorny premise on Rohena Gera’s Sir. This romantic comedy aims to be warm and feel-good, but there’s something uncomfortable about the way it stages its social framework. You don’t have to be woke to see through its nature.
