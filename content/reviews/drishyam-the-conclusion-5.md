It’s entirely natural to question the existence of the third film — not first, not second — of a crime franchise, regardless of box-office success. Especially if the premise revolves and revolves around a movie-mad father who keeps hiding the body of his daughter’s abuser to protect his family. Especially if the first two are Bollywood remakes of superior Malayalam thrillers. Especially if the third movie, which has “conclusion” in its title, has nothing to do with the third Mohanlal starrer (released in May) and insists on charting its own narrative path. How dare we be original? The signs are ominous, not least because the background score of this Hindi film series has been partially responsible for my hearing problems over the years. How much is too much? Rinse, Roar (that’s Singham, the other Ajay-Devgn-in-Goa franchise), Repeat. Do it once and it’s novel; do it twice and it’s suspect; do it thrice and the cash-cow is mooing.
