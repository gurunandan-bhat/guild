There are actors who play multiple characters, and then there is Bhuvan Bam, who seems to have built an entire universe around himself. Don't believe us? Wait for the end credits of Dhindora Season 2. Nearly eight characters have Bhuvan's name attached to them, and that's not all. He is also the show's story and screenplay writer. Actor, writer, performer and creator, Bam is practically running the show single-handedly. And the best part? He makes it look ridiculously easy. With eight episodes, a village full of eccentric characters, jokes and enough family drama to rival any soap, Dhindora 2 is a chaotic but largely fun ride.
