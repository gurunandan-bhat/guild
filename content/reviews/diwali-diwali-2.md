Ritesh Menon’s short-long film, lasting just a little over an hour, is literally like the kind of Diwali mithai which leaves a mildly sweet after-taste which doesn’t linger. You can eat it, and forget about it. Ansh Sikka (Mihir Ahuja) is about to give his parents a surprise. He will be home for Diwali. No advance warning. It’s just the kind of news that would make most desi parents jump for joy. But the Sikkas, daddy (Manoj Pahwa), mommy (Neelu Kohli) and sister (Aurra Bhatnagar Badoni), have a secret: they do not love the fancy high-rise flat Ansh has bought for them, for which they have had to leave their beloved middle-class colony, missing the warmth and joy spread by close-knit neighbours who are like extended family.
