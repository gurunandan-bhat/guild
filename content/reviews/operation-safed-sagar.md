At the end of Operation Safed Sagar, the following words light up the screen: "Operation Safed Sagar remains the highest aerial offensive operation in history. The Indian Air Force flew a total of 7,631 sorties during the Kargil War across its helicopter, transport and fighter squadrons.... (It is) an operation without parallel in the history of aerial warfare". Operation Safed Sagar is also a series with little or no parallel in the history of Indian storytelling. Across six episodes, the Netflix show not only talks about facts and figures, numbers and nuances, but also underlines the emotional depth of an operation driven by both patriotic fervour and personal payback.
