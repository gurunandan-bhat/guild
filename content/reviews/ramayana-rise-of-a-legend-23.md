Tales of the battle between good and evil never get old, and in the case of the Ramayana, it is as old as it gets, and still quite resonant in Indian socio-politics. The characters are well-etched in the nation’s psyche, the turning points are all too familiar and the lessons learned ample. Namit Malhotra’s Ramayana: Part 1 is well too aware of what’s at stake while tackling a Hindu epic that inspires devotion as well as religious fervour. In scale and spectacle, too, there’s no stone unturned, for the film is as imposing as it gets.
