Finally, an Indian epic that stands alongside the world's grandest cinematic spectacles. Nitesh Tiwari delivers a breathtaking cinematic spectacle without ever losing sight of Ramayana’s emotional core. Based on Valmiki’s poem, an ancient Hindu epic, Ramayana: Part 1 chronicles the events leading up to the battle between good and evil, embodied by Ayodhya’s Prince Ram and Lanka’s mighty king Raavan. Blessed with a boon from Brahma, Raavan is seemingly invincible, while Ram, the human incarnation of Lord Vishnu, is destined to be the one who vanquishes him.
