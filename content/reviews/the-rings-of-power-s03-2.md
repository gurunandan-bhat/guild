In 2024, Amazon Prime Video's The Lord Of The Rings: The Rings Of Power showed the fall of Eregion and Sauron's revealing his true identity. Now, two years later, the fantasy adventure series returns for a third season with a five-year time jump, with Sauron (Charlie Vickers) becoming seemingly immovable as he captures most of Middle-earth on his way to forge the One Ring to rule them all. It is up to a select few Dwarves, Elves, Men, and Wizards to try and stop him in the instalment that echoes big The Empire Strikes Back energy.
