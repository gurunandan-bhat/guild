Director-writer Madhumita’s second release this month (after Love Always) is a far more sophisticated film, broaching topics often deemed too tough for our movies to discuss. Despite the hard exterior of an investigative thriller, it’s the dramatic moments that reveal the writer’s sensitivity. This is most evident in a scene where a broken father is called into a mortuary to identify a corpse. Rather than rushing through it for the sake of the larger plot, the film slows down and gives this parent the dignity he deserves. He’s nervous, confused, and hoping against hope in these brief moments. The film is just as invested in the police officer hero, for whom it is impossible to treat such an event as merely part of the job (he vomits when he reaches a crime scene early on).
