Karthik Subbaraj has always insisted that he does not make films. He makes a padam, which, to be clear, is not just a literal Tamil translation of “film”, but is a separate ontological category. It says something hazy about genre, form, formula, and Subbaraj keeps theorising and re-theorising the padam with every new film he makes. Roughly, his padams chase moments of melodramatic coincidence, full of unwieldy ideas and wild insertions of plot, sculpted in camp excess and winking indulgence, with morality always at the centre of the story, where characters will ultimately revel in moral displays of affection.
