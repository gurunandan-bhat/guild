Shyamili Das aka Shy (Aarushi Bajaj) is perpetually angry and ashamed of not being rich. She resents her parents for happily accepting their middle-class fate and believes she deserves pretty privilege. Confident that her wealthy boyfriend Mayank (Piyush Khati) is her ticket to a better life, she is thrown when he dumps her, forcing her to rethink her carefully mapped-out future. Set in the late 1990s and early 2000s, Sreeti Mukerji’s formulaic romcom wears the persona of the Hollywood campus capers of that era, albeit with a desi twist. But unlike the usual underdog stories, you don’t necessarily root for the not-so-likeable central character here. Instead, it’s her nicer rich friends, feminist archrival and quintessential girl’s girl Tara (Bianca Arora), cute love interests and warm parents who win you over.
