The hills are alive with the sound of music. Music as in the mumbling song of an old man, singing in Kumaoni to the gods of the mountains. He smokes beedis as he croons downhill, his ageing wife being carried by two villagers behind. She has to be taken down often for a checkup by the nearest town doctor. As they ride in a mountain taxi (often a Sumo car) to the clinic, the old man can’t help but look at a funeral pyre flaming at the banks of a river. Death, a universal impending fate.
