The concept of CCTV cameras doesn't exist in the world of Haiwaan. Neither does logic, rationale, common sense or coherence. One could still overlook the absence of all these elements if this was a Priyadarshan comedy. But Haiwaan is supposedly a cat-and-mouse game, a play of good vs evil, a thriller which is meant to keep its players on its toes and the minds of its audience ticking. But this is a film that mistakes dumbness for dread and incomprehensible for acuity. In terms of this genre alone, Priyadarshan has done more, and done it much better. Haiwaan is, after all, a remake of the man's own 2016 Malayalam thriller Oppam. And even though it is an almost faithful adaptation of its core material, Haiwaan feels empty and tiring, which Oppam certainly didn't. The presence of big stars and better production values doesn't cover up for the tedious slog this movie is.
