What started as a one-off Malayalam film in 2014 has also become one of the biggest franchises in Hindi. The remake, Drishyam, arrived in 2015, followed by a sequel in 2022. Now, Drishyam: The Conclusion is here and, supposedly, this is the final instalment. It is not easy to give up on a lucrative franchise, so we’ll have to wait and see if that holds true.
