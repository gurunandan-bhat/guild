Because somewhere along the way, we have completely accepted the Salgaonkars as our people. Yes, we know Vijay has done something wrong and, technically, he should face the law. But director Abhishek Pathak makes us look at the story from Vijay's side. We are not sitting in the theatre hoping the police win. We are waiting to see how Vijay gets out of the next situation. Tabu returns as Meera Deshmukh, and this time her frustration has reached another level. She hasn't been able to get Vijay punished, and that anger turns personal. She wants revenge. Enter the SP Crime Branch, played by Jaideep Ahlawat. And this is not simply the presence of an actor. This is the presence of a star. Every time he appears on screen, there is an authority and intensity that immediately changes the atmosphere.
