"Poori duniya hi jhooti hai (The whole world lies)." There is a line in Student of the Year: After Dark that pretty much sums up the world this show wants to create. And, honestly, it isn't entirely wrong. Everyone is in a race to win, and when the stakes are high, morality, friendship and basic humanity can take a backseat. The real question is: how far will you go to get what you want? The original Student of the Year, with Varun Dhawan, Alia Bhatt and Sidharth Malhotra leading the campus race, gave us a rather PG version of ambition, popularity, youthful rivalry and relationships. Its latest instalment, SOTY After Dark, wants to take things several shades darker. This time, the Gen Z crowd comes armed with secrets, sinister social media pages, messy relationships and a murder mystery. Except, somewhere between wanting to be a campus drama and a psychological thriller, the show loses its plot. Quite literally, at times.
