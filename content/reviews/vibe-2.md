I have a theory after watching Vibe, and I think we're witnessing something interesting in Bollywood right now: almost every male actor secretly wants to be an action hero. Think about it. Shah Rukh Khan was the ultimate lover boy, the man who could make romance his biggest weapon. But today, even he has moved towards action. Yash has built his image around larger-than-life action, while actors like Sonu Sood and Kunal Kemmu have gone a step further and directed themselves in action films. And Kunal is one of the funniest actors we have! Maybe there's something about action that actors simply can't resist. Romance can make you a star, but action lets you build an entire world around yourself. And honestly, audiences don't seem to mind who is doing it, as long as the film entertains.
