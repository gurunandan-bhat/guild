The new Marvel Cinematic Universe movie Spider-Man: Brand New Day is a mixed bag. It’s both conventional and kinetic, sombre and silly. Under the stewardship of director Destin Daniel Cretton, the movie isn’t as synthetic as the franchise’s previous instalments — especially the previous two films starring Tom Holland as Spider-Man. Brand New Day is more grounded than those movies, less bothered by multiverses and mayhem and more concerned with the characters. The action is spectacular (and most definitely inspired by the popular Spider-Man video games by Insomniac), the superhero shenanigans are reliably rote, but Brand New Day is at its best when it’s being a Bollywood emotional drama from the mid-2000s
