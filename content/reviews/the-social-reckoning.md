Sixteen years ago, The Social Network succinctly captured a fledgling Facebook making its journey from campus dorm to corporate corridor via courtroom, with Aaron Sorkin's razor-sharp screenplay and David Fincher's focused direction exploring universal questions of social media connection and subsequent human isolation. Jesse Eisenberg played Facebook founder Mark Zuckerberg as both arrogant opportunist and conflicted underdog, with the film being regarded as a modern classic. The Social Network was kinetic and full of kick, capturing the zeitgeist of Facebook’s inception and the double deals that brought it into millions of homes.
