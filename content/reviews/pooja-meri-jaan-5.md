Mrunal Thakur and Huma Qureshi starrer legal-drama Pooja Meri Jaan--centered around a girl who gets entangled in an abetment case after her jilted lover commits suicide—drops amid powerful context. In the aftermath of the gangrape of a minor girl in a park in Delhi, central universities are erupting in protests. Women are out with placards, bearing catchy, diss couplets. How to really trust men when your feed is filled with videos of one of them forcefully picking a girl up with his hand between her thighs. The movie also comes post the Atul Subhash suicide case—about a techie who killed himself over allegations of mental harassment by his estranged wife and in-laws—and multiple incidents of women murdering their husbands and going-to-be husbands.
