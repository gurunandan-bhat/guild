"Alapara Kelaparom. Thalaivar nirandharam." These lines from Hukum, the chartbuster from Jailer, loosely translate to unleashing mayhem and declaring the Thalaivar eternal. They don't just capture Rajinikanth's larger-than-life aura; they also sum up the enduring appeal of a superstar who continues to command the big screen. Directing a sequel to a hit film is one thing. Following up a Rajinikanth blockbuster that grossed Rs 600 crore worldwide is an entirely different ball game. For Nelson Dilipkumar, Jailer 2 is a Himalayan task, with the filmmaker attempting to recreate the swagger, humour and mass appeal of its predecessor. He largely reaches the summit, even if the journey takes a few detours.
