Nimisha Sajayan in Babita Singh ReportingIn 2021, The Great Indian Kitchen — a genre-defying, gender dynamic-defining Malayalam film — brought patriarchy to the forefront in ways both casual and conscious, with its scathing social commentary highlighting the invisible, exhausting and unacknowledged labour in the domestic space that most women in our kitchens, and outside it, are still subjected to. Nimisha Sajayan walked away with the spotlight in the film — symbolised by that triumphant walkout at the end — with her character, who is simply credited as "The Wife", reclaiming autonomy and rejecting the status quo. In many ways, The Great Indian Kitchen, directed by Jeo Baby, feels like a companion piece to Babita Singh Reporting, the new Prime Video film, that stars Sajayan as its protagonist.
