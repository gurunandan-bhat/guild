In Manali, a suspended IPS officer, Sikander (Arjun Rampal), accidentally hits two friends, Vicky and Shaan, with his car. When Vicky, a local cafe owner, goes missing from the hospital, Sikander helps Shaan search for him, only to get entangled with his estranged wife and fellow cop, Yamini (Priyamani), in a game of betrayal, a missing fortune and deadly criminals. None of the performances really land. Arjun Rampal is an actor with limitations and needs a team that knows how to work around them. Otherwise, his supposedly suave, cold persona can feel like a chunk of wood. Priyamani  stays focused in a limited role, but the character has neither context nor a backstory. She is far too good a talent to be wasting herself in random outings like these. Others, including Nakul Sahdev, Rohit Chandel, Shayank Shukla and Noyrika Bhateja, look too listless to salvage the film from its mediocrity.
