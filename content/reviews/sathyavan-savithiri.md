The legend of Sathyavan and Savithiri is a story of love, devotion and a wife's determination to bring her husband back from the clutches of death. When Yama, the god of death, arrives to claim Sathyavan's life, Savithiri refuses to give up. She follows him, challenges him with her wit and eventually wins her husband's life back. Director Praveen S Vijaay's Sathyavan Savithiri borrows from this familiar mythological tale but gives it a courtroom twist.
