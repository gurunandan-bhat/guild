On the surface, its veneer of sophistication is impressively consistent but spectacle frequently overshadows substance in Ramayana. The film's gargantuan scale is the first thing that strikes the audience. It remains, for the most part, the only thing that stands out. The film surges forward relentlessly on the back of eye-popping technical wizardry, sustained visual flourish and elaborately mounted, CGI-bolstered battle sequences. The surface gloss satiates the senses all right but it wears one down when a surfeit of it leads to monotony and overwhelms the story.
