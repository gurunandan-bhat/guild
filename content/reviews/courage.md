The title is self-explanatory and the lines, ‘...but one in the loneliness saw the sun’, from the opening song prepare you for more of the same dose of relentless resilience. In the early frames of the movie, when a friend tells the female lead, Rani, that her bravery is no less than that of Rani of Jhansi, you think this sure is misplaced hyperbole. But as the narrative progresses in different timelines, you learn that he was not exaggerating. Rani (Sanskruti Balgude) sure has nerves of steel and a steelier resolve. If the very first scenes open in the US, where Jaydeep (Sharib Hashmi) suddenly falls unconscious, we are soon taken back to their college romance in Bombay in 1974. Cut to 1983, when they meet again after a gap of nearly 10 years. She has an abusive marriage behind her and Jaydeep has lost a kidney.
