Based on a 2011 novel of the same name, Wildwood is animated by the distinctive Laika studio’s motion-capture style, with an A-list star cast doing the voice-work. It follows brave teenager Prue (Peyton Elizabeth Lee) who goes into the dark woods in search of her infant brother, kidnapped by a murder of crows. She is not alone: for company, she has her pal Curtis (Jacob Tremblay), who, naturally, has a crush on her. As the twosome forges deeper into the forest, they begin encountering a whole host of creatures– fearsome coyotes, helpful birds, cute rats– along with several other magical, mystical people. There is the grieving dowager governess Alexandra (Carey Mulligan) who hasn’t got over the loss of her own young son. Armed by her expertise of the Black Arts in her quest to get her little boy back, will she help Prue and Curtis? Or does she harbour ill-will towards the two youngsters in her domain? There is an ancient crone with special powers, who is the keeper of many secrets.
