There’s a special kind of high that only a really good film can give you. But when it comes from watching one of Tamil cinema's favourite stars finally have the kind of year you’ve been rooting for, the excitement hits differently. And when that star is Suriya, in a film helmed by Aavesham director Jithu Madhavan, alongside Nazriya and Naslen, the joy is almost impossible to contain. Scene, Suriya's latest film, delivers exactly that rush - the kind that makes you grin, cheer and walk out feeling like the star is finally back in his element.
