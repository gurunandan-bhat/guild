A David versus Goliath story from 1977 gets the big-screen treatment with Apple TV's Being Heumann. Disability rights activist Judy Heumann held the longest sit-in protest in US history at San Francisco's Federal Building in 1977. She was fighting for equal rights for thousands of disabled citizens like herself as they pushed Washington, DC, to listen to their demands. Award-winning filmmaker Siân Heder turns the spotlight on this incredible true tale that both educates and moves you with its heartfelt story. Being Heummann authentically casts several disabled actors in these key roles, which brings home the rousing true-life story.
