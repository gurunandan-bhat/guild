The Hunger Games Sunrise on the Reaping review: The biggest question, when confronted with yet another edition of a long-running franchise is quite simply this: will it tell us something we didn’t already know? Followed up with: did it do the telling well? On neither of those counts does Sunrise on the Reaping, the 6th instalment of the series — sequel to the 2023 Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, and prequel to 2012 The Hunger Games — pass muster. Set 24 years before the first story, and based on the 2025 novel by Suzanne Collins, about the only saving grace in this new film is Joseph Zada, who plays a young Haymitch Abernathy with feeling. The rest is a snoozefest.
