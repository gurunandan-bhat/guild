There are two kinds of Indian journalism movies. Neither of them is nice — which perhaps reflects the state of journalism itself — but it is what it is. The first kind of movie unfolds with the confidence of having spoken to one former reporter as research; it explains the profession like a teacher in third grade because it’s also learning simultaneously. The second kind of movie unfolds with the confidence of having spoken to several reporters from different newsrooms, past and present, so that this research can be reduced to a journalism-for-dummies template; it, too, explains the profession like a teacher in third grade because it thinks that the viewers are learning something they’ve never heard of before.
