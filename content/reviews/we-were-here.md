“Sometimes, I wish he was normal, like everyone else,” a young man (Shubham Kujur) says about his father, Mr. Pimple (Devendra Dodke), while looking into the camera. A member of a staunch anti-AI (Artificial Intelligence) group, along with his friends – Bagchi (Satish Kashyap) and Ganguly (Arindol Bagchi) – Pimple Sr. has recently begun to identify as a microwave. The wicked subversion in Pranav Bhasin’s mockumentary, We Were Here (2026), lies in the fact that in an earlier scene the son comes out to his father as gay. A dialogue one would expect to hear from a conservative parent is instead enunciated by his worried son. This is just one of the dozen sparkling flourishes in Bhasin’s film. In its runtime of a shade under 11 mins, it condenses a pressing anxiety of our times. As machines replace human beings, what must our plan of action be? More importantly, is there anything we can do?
