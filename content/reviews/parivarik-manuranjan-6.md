Parivarik ManuRanjan, the new release on SonyLIV, wins you over from the first frame. Set in Lucknow, the film features an unlikely pair—the ever-affable Pankaj Tripathi and the gorgeous Aditi Rao Hydari—playing the leads. They are not a pair one would usually think of, and yet once you see them sharing screen space, you almost say, “Why not?” Perhaps the film’s biggest draw is its lead pair. Coupled with a feel-good premise, this slice-of-life film with a dash of romance thrown in seems like a nice comfort watch for the weekend. But is the film able to cash in on its actors and unusual casting? Let’s find out.
