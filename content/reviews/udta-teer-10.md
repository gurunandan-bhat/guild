A few years back, Ayushmann Khurrana created a genre for himself by playing the everyday man in numerous slice-of-life comedies. Khurrrana has had a few releases in the last two years, but none have been able to put him back in the spot he had created and ruled (at the box office) for a considerable amount of time. Udta Teer, directed by Aakash Kaushik, brings Khurrana back to his comedic elements. While he doesn’t play a regular guy with relatable problems, Udta Teer still can be put in a genre of comedies that the actor has aced in the past. In his latest film, Khurrana plays a Pakistani spy in India, who suffers post trauma memory loss, which leads to complexities for the neighbour state. The story, also written by Kaushik, has the perfect ingredients to make for a roaring entertainer. Does it deliver? Let's find out.
