Based on Colleen Hoover's bestselling novel of the same name, the film has been directed by Michael Showalter, who keeps the suspense alive through a steady stream of twists, secrets and revelations. Dakota Johnson plays Lowen Ashleigh, a struggling writer who gets an unexpected opportunity when she is hired to complete the bestselling series of Verity Crawford, a successful author who is now bedridden and unable to continue writing. Verity's husband Jeremy and her publishers want Lowen to take over the series, but there's a catch: she has to move into the Crawford family home in Vermont and go through Verity's work — her manuscripts, notes, documents and everything she has left behind.
